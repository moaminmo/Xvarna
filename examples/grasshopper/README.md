# Grasshopper examples

These tracked `.ghx` onboarding documents are generated by `eng/generate-gh-examples.ps1` against the real Rhino 8 Grasshopper SDK and the current XVARNA assembly. They are host-loadable documents, not screenshots or fake XML.

- `01-xvarna-preflight.ghx`: engine, quality, units, device, cache, scheduler, and diagnostics setup.
- `02-vayu-scene-visibility.ghx`: scene lifecycle, portable backend, runtime telemetry, and flagship visibility analyses.
- `03-hvare-daylight-solar.ghx`: optical materials, daylight/Radiance validation, solar scenarios, and freeform envelope.
- `04-vahman-study-report.ghx`: versioned study workspace, optimization, atomic commit, and portable reporting.
- `05-rashnu-evidence-multifidelity.ghx`: Evidence Passport, estimator-valid global sensitivity, and cost-aware Fast/Reference scheduling.
- `06-urban-sun-hours.ghx`: urban sun exposure, first-blocker attribution, and viewport highlighting.
- `07-parametric-shade-delta.ghx`: static/dynamic scene updates, refit/rebuild telemetry, and solar deltas.
- `08-target-weighted-green-view.ghx`: direct Target/Weighted/Green View workflow.
- `09-facade-privacy.ghx`: directed intervisibility, facade exposure, and privacy risk.
- `10-annual-daylight-reference.ghx`: sDA/ASE/UDI Fast Path, Radiance export, and validation.
- `11-dynamic-observer-corridor.ghx`: observer path, protected corridor, 3D isovist, and landmark analysis.
- `12-end-to-end-decision-report.ghx`: manifest-to-Pareto-to-Evidence-to-report decision workflow.

The templates deliberately do not embed project geometry, weather data, or design variables. Reference your Rhino geometry and connect the inputs described by each component; the panels preserve the intended workflow without silently inventing architectural assumptions.
