# XVARNA 1.0 Review Candidate — completion contract

## Outcome

One command, `eng/build-review-candidate.ps1`, produces the exact archive that can be sent to external reviewers. It contains Rhino 8 and Rhino 9 packages, CLI/SDK, package and nested checksums, release manifest, CycloneDX SBOM, Phase 1/2 automated evidence, reviewer protocol, beta KPI/sign-off records, claims/benchmark method and three case-study protocols.

## Engineering-complete before sending

- 49 GH1 and 49 GH2 semantic capabilities, all documented and represented in 12 example canvases.
- Rust/.NET/native/Radiance/GPU automated checks and package construction.
- Deterministic S/M/L corpus generator; Smoke or Full evidence profile.
- Typed Parquet for performance/parity and daylight Fast/Reference pairs.
- Security threat model, dependency/content license procedure, rollback/revocation and community policy.
- Preprint-ready technical report structure, citation metadata and evidence-bound launch copy.
- A machine-readable `review-readiness.json` that never converts missing human evidence into PASS.

## Intentionally collected after sending

- Interactive install/place/wire/solve/save/reopen on reviewers’ Rhino installations.
- AMD hardware, external CPU-only/device-loss/long-soak matrix.
- 20 testers, 10 projects and measured beta KPIs.
- Three independent sign-offs and real-model case-study licenses/hashes/results.
- Trademark/legal owner decisions, artifact signing, antivirus/clean-install checks.
- Fourteen-day RC soak, DOI, public website/video/screenshots and owner go/no-go.
- GitHub/Yak/Rhino Food4Rhino publication and post-release verification.

Those are external validation/publication acts, not missing product features. They remain mandatory for the original Phase 2/3 `PASS` and public `v1.0.0`.
