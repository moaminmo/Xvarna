# Code of Conduct

XVARNA is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and inclusive
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Disclose uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest.
- Welcome users with different disciplines and experience levels.
- Protect private project data and unpublished research.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and publication of private reports or data are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project lead or, once available, the
designated conduct contact. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a
warning, suspend participation, or permanently ban a participant when needed
to protect the community.
