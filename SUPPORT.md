# Support policy

XVARNA `1.0.0-rc.1` is a review candidate. Use the bug template for reproducible defects and include a redacted `XV Diagnostics` bundle. Use discussions for workflow questions once the public repository is enabled. Security issues must follow `SECURITY.md` and must not be filed publicly.

Supported review hosts are Windows x64 with Rhino 8.20+ and Grasshopper 1, or Rhino 9 with Grasshopper 1/2. The native CPU backend is the correctness baseline; GPU support depends on the adapter and driver reported by `XV Devices`. Review support is best-effort until public 1.0, and no architectural, engineering, daylight-certification, or legal outcome should depend solely on the software without appropriate professional review.
