# XVARNA 0.14.0 — DAENA Spatial and Solar Intelligence

XVARNA 0.14 closes the requested DAENA/HVARE spatial-intelligence milestone.

## Highlights

- Equal-solid-angle material-aware 3D isovists with radial volume/surface measures, openness, convergence, complete ray fields, general top-K attribution, and exact category partitions.
- Exact remove-one re-tracing for leading blockers, including recovered visible weight and volumetric delta.
- Camera-aware partial Landmark Visibility using spherical apparent solid angle and deterministic projected-disk sampling.
- Aligned spatial scenario comparison with volume, openness, mean-radius, and per-object attribution deltas.
- Validated object-assigned visible/solar material system with deterministic multi-layer transmission and loss attribution.
- Full solar material-scenario timelines, ranked object/category loss, and candidate-minus-baseline deltas.
- Early-massing vertical-column Solar/Shading Envelope with weighted access/shading thresholds and controlling sensor/time provenance.
- Direct Rhino viewport occluder highlighting by exact DAENA/HVARE Object ID.
- Four new CLI workflows, additive C/.NET APIs, and seven Rhino 8/9 Grasshopper components.

## Grasshopper components

`XV Materials`, `XV Isovist 3D`, `XV Landmark`, `XV Compare`, `XV Solar Scenarios`, `XV Solar Envelope`, and `XV Highlight` advance the identical Rhino 8/.NET 8 and Rhino 9/.NET 10 connector surface to 33 components.
