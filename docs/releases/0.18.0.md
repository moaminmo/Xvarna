# XVARNA 0.18.0 — Scientific evidence and multi-fidelity research engine

Status: local engineering release candidate; not remotely published.

0.18 turns the completed Study platform into an auditable research workflow:

- RASHNU Evidence Passport with result/scene/input hashes, method and fidelity identity, backend/device/tool versions, seed, samples, runtime, assumptions, limitations, citations, parity/reference evidence, and mutation-detecting BLAKE3 seal;
- generated Saltelli/Jansen and Morris global-sensitivity experiments with exact row alignment, method-layout validation, deterministic seeds, physical-unit elementary effects, and hash-linked results;
- weighted robust-scenario mean/deviation/worst/CVaR and chance-constraint violation probability;
- uncertainty-aware feasibility and conservative interval-Pareto fronts;
- aligned Fast/Reference discrepancy calibration with intercept, slope, residual σ, R², propagated observation noise, two-objective Monte-Carlo EHVI, and cost-aware Reference selection;
- additive Rust, six-function C ABI, typed .NET 8/10, six-command CLI, and `XV Evidence`, `XV Sensitivity+`, `XV Fidelity` Grasshopper surfaces;
- tracked analytic fixtures and a full-stack smoke gate integrated into `eng/check.ps1`.

The acquisition policy is deliberately named for what it implements. It does not claim qNEHVI, MF-HVKG, a Gaussian process, or joint-batch fantasies. External architectural projects, a representative competitor corpus, AMD evidence, and independent review remain public-release gates.
