# XVARNA 0.6.0 — MEHR Annual Irradiance

Released 2026-08-31 as an engineering milestone toward the 1.0 flagship.

## Product release

- Strict hourly/subhourly EPW ingestion with midpoint UTC semantics, weather diagnostics, and BLAKE3 identity.
- NREL SPA plus EnergyPlus-style Perez anisotropic tilted-surface irradiance.
- Direct, dome, circumsolar, horizon, sky-diffuse, ground-reflected, global, peak, and annual energy outputs.
- 144 dome and 24 horizon rays per sensor plus closest-hit direct/circumsolar visibility.
- First-hit object/instance/mesh/triangle/distance timeline and dominant lost-energy/static-sky attribution.
- Cooperative cancellation and exact progress through Rust, C ABI, and .NET 8/.NET 10.
- `XV Irradiance` for Rhino 8 and Rhino 9 with heatmap, annual outputs, and sensor-major timeline trees.
- `xvarna annual-irradiance` with human/JSON reports and complete component CSV export.
- Public `EpwWeatherFile`, sync/async/job .NET API and additive `0.6.x` C ABI.

## Validation

The release includes analytic Perez, ground-reflection, open-sky, full-blocker, first-hit attribution, cancellation, ABI-layout, managed-runtime, and EPW time/missing-data tests. Scientific equations, provenance, acceptance cases, and non-claims are recorded in `docs/validation/annual-irradiance.md`.
