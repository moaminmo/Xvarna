# Beta data and consent

Participation is voluntary. XVARNA runs locally and the review build contains no product telemetry uploader. Reviewers choose what to send. The requested record is limited to host/hardware versions, task timing, success/failure, issue severity and redacted diagnostics.

Do not submit personal data, client names, coordinates, proprietary geometry, weather files, screenshots or reports unless you are authorized to share them. A reviewer may allow anonymous aggregate metrics without allowing publication of their model. Case-study permission is separate, revocable until publication, and must name the exact files and license.

Maintainers should store the consent record separately from anonymized metrics, replace names with session IDs, restrict raw access, and delete withdrawn or unnecessary raw submissions. Security reports follow `SECURITY.md`.
